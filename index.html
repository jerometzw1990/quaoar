Capabilities

The full compliance lifecycle,
under one practice.

From first risk assessment to ongoing monitoring, we cover everything a regulated business needs to onboard customers with confidence — without building a compliance department from scratch.

KYC Program Design

Risk-based customer due diligence frameworks, onboarding workflows, EDD triggers and approval matrices — documented, board-ready and proportionate to your licence obligations.

KYB & UBO Mapping

Corporate onboarding for complex structures: registry verification, multi-layer ownership charts, ultimate beneficial owner identification and member-level KYC across jurisdictions.

Screening Frameworks

Sanctions, PEP and adverse media screening calibrated to your risk appetite — list selection, fuzzy-match thresholds, alert handling procedures and ongoing monitoring cadence.

Transaction Monitoring

Scenario and rule design for fiat and crypto flows, threshold tuning, STR/SAR escalation playbooks and wallet screening procedures that stand up to regulator scrutiny.

Remediation & Lookbacks

Backlog clearance, file remediation and historical transaction reviews delivered by an experienced analyst bench — with audit trails your examiners can follow end to end.

Vendor Selection & Integration

Independent evaluation of KYC/AML tooling, RFP support and implementation oversight — we configure the platform around your policy, not the other way around.

How we engage

A defined path from exposure
to assurance.

Every engagement follows the same disciplined sequence, whether we're building a program from zero or repairing one under regulatory pressure.

Assess

Enterprise-wide risk assessment, gap analysis against your licence conditions, and a prioritised findings register within the first two weeks.

Design

Policies, procedures, risk-scoring models and escalation matrices drafted to your regulator's expectations — reviewed with your board and MLRO.

Implement

Workflow configuration, vendor integration, analyst training and parallel-run testing until the program performs under real volume.

Monitor

Periodic reviews, independent testing, regulatory horizon scanning and ongoing tuning so the program stays effective as your business grows.
